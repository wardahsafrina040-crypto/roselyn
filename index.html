My Reflection

Building this portfolio website was one of the most unexpectedly meaningful things I've done this semester. I went in thinking it was just another assignment, but somewhere between choosing colors and writing about myself in English, it became something I actually cared about. The hardest part wasn't the code — it was figuring out how to describe who I am and what I'm capable of, honestly and professionally, in a language that isn't my first.

This semester taught me that IT is more than just typing code — it's about thinking clearly, structuring information, and communicating ideas. From designing a database in Basis Data class to building logic in Pemrograman Dasar, I've started to see how each subject connects. Going forward, I want to deepen my understanding of web development, get more comfortable with Python, and keep documenting my growth — because looking back at where I started is what pushes me to keep going.
